Course conditions app
Prototype — demo only
Nothing here is live or connected to a real database. I've used Western Gailes because it's familiar to you — it's really just to show what a finished product might look like.
For the golfer
After finishing their round, a player scans a code at the 18th green, the clubhouse bar, locker room — wherever. It opens a simple form on their phone. No app to download, no account required. They rate the greens, fairways, bunkers and overall experience in under 90 seconds, and earn points redeemable as pro shop vouchers. The better the data they give, the more the platform is worth to everyone.

For the greenkeeper
Every submission feeds into a private dashboard only you can see. Instead of hearing about problems second-hand, you get structured, timestamped feedback from real players — greens speed trending down, fairways soft on the back nine, bunkers consistently well rated. The dashboard shows what's improving, what isn't, and what players are flagging for your attention. It doesn't replace your judgement — it just means you're working with more information than you had before.

Would this dashboard actually be useful to you day to day — or does it tell you things you already know?
