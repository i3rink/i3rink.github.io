What you're looking at
Two pages built specifically to show you the concept:

**For the golfer**
After finishing their round, a player scans a code (either at the 18th green, the clubhouse bar, locker room etc). It opens a simple form on their phone — no app to download, no account required first. They rate the greens, fairways, bunkers and overall experience, take less than 90 seconds, and in return for their feedback they earn points redeemable as pro shop vouchers. The better the data they give, the more the platform is worth to everyone.

**For the greenkeeper**
Every submission feeds into a private dashboard only you guys see. Instead of hearing about problems second-hand, you get structured, timestamped feedback from real players — greens speed trending down, fairways soft on the back nine, bunkers consistently well rated. The dashboard shows you what's improving, what isn't, and what players are specifically flagging for your attention. It doesn't replace your judgement — it just means you're potentially working with more information than you had before.
